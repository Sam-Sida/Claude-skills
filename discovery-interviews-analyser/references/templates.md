# Output templates

## Per-interview analysis

```markdown
# Interview analysis: P<n>

| Field | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Study | <study name> |
| Date | <interview date, if known> |
| Participant | P<n>: <role, company type or size, and product usage> |
| Interviewer | <initials or role> |
| Source | <transcript file> |

## 0. Snapshot

- **TL;DR:** 3 bullets with the most important takeaways.

## 1. General Context & Independent Pain Points

*The user's life and workflow outside our product.*

- **Topic analysis:** the high-level business or workflow challenges they face.
- **Sentiment:** overall stance (**overwhelmed**, **resigned**, or **proactive**) and why.
- **Voice of the Customer:** 2–3 "gold" quotes that define their primary struggle.

| Topic | Description | Sentiment | Quote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| | | -1 Negative | "…" (12:34) |

> **Researcher's Note:** …

## 2. Current Product Experience

*Their interaction with our existing features and services.*

- **Positive sentiment topics:** what works well, and where the "magic" is.
- **Negative sentiment topics:** where the friction, counter-intuitive UX, or failure is.
- **Voice of the Customer:**
  - 3 verbatim positive quotes.
  - 3 verbatim negative quotes about current features.

| Feature | Description | Sentiment | Quote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

> **Researcher's Note:** …

## 3. Concept & Proposition Testing

*Their reaction to the new ideas or prototypes presented.*

- **Validated concepts (+):** which ideas resonated, and why.
- **Rejected or low-value concepts (-):** which ideas they ignored or explicitly disliked.
- **Inquiry:** specific questions they asked about the roadmap or how the concept works.

| Concept | Description | Feedback & Inquiry | Prompted? | Sentiment | Quote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

> **Researcher's Note:** …

## 4. Innovation & "Blue Sky" Ideas

*The user's own suggestions beyond our current exploration.*

- **Feature requests:** entirely new capabilities they explicitly asked for.
- **Unmet needs:** opportunities they mentioned that we don't address yet.
- **Voice of the Customer:** 1–2 quotes where they describe their "dream" solution.

| Product idea | Description | Unmet need | Quote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

> **Researcher's Note:** …

## 5. Top 5 Questions from the Interviewee

1. "…" (mm:ss): *what it reveals*

> **Researcher's Note:** …

## Appendix: Interview quality (optional)

- Leading questions that may have biased answers, with the location of each.
- Topics the guide missed or should probe next time.
```
