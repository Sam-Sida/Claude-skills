# Voice of Customer report template

Copy this structure. Fill every placeholder, keep the section numbers, and remove the guidance in italics.

```markdown
# Voice of Customer Report

| Field | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Company | <Company name> |
| Product | <Product name or category> |
| Total reviews analyzed | <usable reviews> (of <total rows>; <excluded> excluded: <reasons>) |
| Sources | <e.g. App Store, Trustpilot, or NPS survey> |
| Date range | <from – to> |
| Average rating | <x.x / 5, if available> |
| Question answered | <the business question, if given> |

## Headline findings

- 3–5 bullets, each with a number.

## 1. Overview of Key Themes

From the analysis of <N> reviews, the following key themes emerged:

| Theme | Percentage of mentions | Sentiment (+ / 0 / −) | Example feedback |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <Theme 1> | <X%> | <a% / b% / c%> | "<verbatim quote>" (<review ID or date>) |

*Themes are multi-label, so percentages can add up to more than 100%.*

## 2. Sentiment Analysis

How customers feel about the product overall, with positive, neutral, and negative trends.

| Sentiment | Percentage of reviews | Comments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Positive | <X%> | *Recurring praise, e.g. quality, effectiveness, or satisfaction.* |
| Neutral | <X%> | *Mixed feedback or no strong opinion, often about secondary aspects.* |
| Negative | <X%> | *Common complaints and what they are about.* |

*If ratings exist:* rating vs text sentiment, and the share of mismatches.
*If dates exist:* the sentiment trend by period, and what changed.

## 3. Topic Categorization

Feedback grouped by category, to pinpoint where customers are satisfied or dissatisfied.

| Category | % of reviews | Sentiment (+ / 0 / −) | Key insight | Representative quote |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <e.g. Product Quality> | | | | |

*If segments exist:* where sentiment differs by segment, source, or region.

## 4. Actionable Insights

Recommendations based on the themes and sentiment, ranked by priority.

| # | Recommendation | Evidence | Priority | Owner | KPI to track |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

1. **<Actionable insight 1: suggested improvement>**
   - **Insight:** the recurring feedback or sentiment behind this suggestion, with its numbers.
   - **Action:** a specific, practical change.
   - **Customer need:** the need this addresses.

2. **<Actionable insight 2: suggested improvement>**
   - **Insight:** …
   - **Action:** …
   - **Customer need:** …

3. **<Actionable insight 3: suggested improvement>**
   - **Insight:** …
   - **Action:** …
   - **Customer need:** …

## 5. Summary

- **Overall satisfaction:** the overall sentiment in one or two sentences.
- **Strengths to protect:** …
- **Areas to improve:** …
- **Call to action:** e.g. further analysis, a product update, or a follow-up study.

## Methodology

- Codebook: themes and their one-line definitions.
- Coding: multi-label themes and one overall sentiment per review; quality check on <n> random rows with <x> corrections.
- Limits: sample size, sources, languages, and any bias in who leaves feedback.
```
